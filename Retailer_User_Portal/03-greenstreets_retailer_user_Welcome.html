Getting started

Welcome to Greenstreets, Sarah

You've been added as a Retailer User (a Compliance Manager role) for Primark Stores Ltd. Let's get you oriented in four quick steps — your dashboard, your suppliers, your products, and how to request the data you need. It takes about a minute, and you can revisit it any time from Settings.

1

Get to know your dashboard Step 1

See your live PPWR conformity at a glance — Declarations of Conformity, PFAS substance compliance and technical documentation — and learn where everything lives in the left menu.

2

Monitor your suppliers Step 2

Track who has submitted and who's overdue, and send reminders to keep suppliers moving. We'll point out exactly where.

3

Review your products Step 3

Every SKU shows its packaging completeness. We'll show you how to spot the products that still need supplier data, what the red and amber flags mean, and how to nudge a supplier from the list.

4

Request a Declaration of Conformity Step 4

When a component is missing data, create a scoped DoC request and send it straight to the supplier. We'll walk you through the form.
