Getting started

Welcome to Greenstreets, Keith

Let's get your compliance workspace ready in four quick steps. Bring your team on board, add the suppliers who make your packaging, import your products, and we'll show you around — so you can start collecting the data you need for your EPR & PPWR obligations. No organisation setup needed — that's already done for you.

1

Add your colleagues Step 1

Start by inviting the people you work with as Retailer Admins or Retailer Users, so your whole team can collaborate on compliance. We'll walk you through it step by step.

2

Add your suppliers Step 2

Add the suppliers who make your packaging and invite them to submit their data. We'll guide you through adding your first ones.

3

Import your products Step 3

You can bulk-import your whole product catalogue from a CSV / XLSX, then link each product to the suppliers who make its packaging. We'll show you where.

4

Find your way around Step 4

Finally, we'll point out the left-hand menu so you know how to reach every page — Dashboard, Users, Suppliers, Products, Packagings and Documents.
