## Executive Summary: The Core Paradigm Shift

Under the previous **Directive 94/62/EC**, packaging compliance was largely handled downstream via national EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) fee declarations, with minimal enforcement of essential requirements. 

Under **Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR)** (published in OJ L on 22 January 2025; entered into force on 11 February 2025; **applicable from 12 August 2026** per Article 71):
1. **Direct Applicability:** As an EU Regulation, it is directly binding across all 27 EU Member States without national transposition variance.
2. **Mandatory CE-Style Conformity (Module A):** Every distinct packaging format placed on the EU market must undergo a formal conformity assessment under **Annex VII (Internal Production Control)**.
3. **The DoC is the Legal Capstone:** The Declaration of Conformity under **Annex VIII** is a legally binding assertion of sole liability. A DoC without an underlying, verifiable **Technical Dossier** constitutes a formal regulatory breach under **Article 62(1)** subject to sales bans, mandatory product withdrawals, and administrative fines.
4. **Packaging ID vs. Product ID:** A product SKU is **not** a packaging identifier. Declarations must identify the packaging unit, its components, and its exact material specification.
5. **Retention Window:** Must be kept readily accessible for market surveillance authorities for **5 years** (single-use packaging) or **10 years** (reusable packaging) pursuant to Annex VII, point 4.
