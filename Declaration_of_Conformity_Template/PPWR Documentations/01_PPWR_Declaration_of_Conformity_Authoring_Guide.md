# Authoring Guide: EU Declaration of Conformity under PPWR
**Standard Operating Procedure for Drafting Compliant Annex VIII Declarations**

---

## 1. Legal Mandate & Framework

Under **Article 39** and **Annex VIII** of **Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR)**, any packaging placed on the European Union market must be accompanied by an EU Declaration of Conformity (DoC). 

The DoC is not an informal technical summary or marketing guarantee; it is a **legally binding instrument** governed by EU harmonisation law. By signing the DoC, the manufacturer assumes full civil and administrative liability that the packaging satisfies all applicable requirements in Articles 5 through 12.

### Statutory Validity Preconditions
An EU Declaration of Conformity is legally invalid if:
1. It is not backed by an existing, audit-ready **Technical Dossier (Annex VII)** compiled *prior* to placing the packaging on the market.
2. It lacks any of the mandatory 8 items defined in Annex VIII.
3. It conflates the packaged product SKU with the unique packaging specification ID.
4. It is signed by an unauthorized individual without corporate legal authority.

---

## 2. Line-by-Line Drafting Rules for Annex VIII

The PPWR mandates an 8-item structure. Each item has a precise legal function:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ANNEX VIII MODEL EU DECLARATION OF CONFORMITY                      |
|                                                                                   |
| 1. Packaging Identification Number / Code                                         |
| 2. Manufacturer & Authorised Representative Information                           |
| 3. Statement of Sole Responsibility                                              |
| 4. Object of the Declaration (Traceable description, components, materials)       |
| 5. Conformity Statement (Regulation (EU) 2025/40 & other EU Acts)                 |
| 6. Harmonised Standards, Common Specifications & Technical Specifications        |
| 7. Notified Body Assessment (where applicable)                                    |
| 8. Additional Information, Place & Date of Issue, Authorized Signatory            |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### Item 1: Unique Identification of the Packaging
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `No … (unique identification of the packaging)`
* **Drafting Instruction:** Must assign a distinct identification number, alphanumeric code, or barcode that identifies the **packaging system or component**, **NOT** the end product being sold.
* **Format Best Practice:** Use an internal packaging specification number, packaging BOM code, or packaging GTIN (Global Trade Item Number).
* **Common Error:** Putting the consumer product SKU (e.g., `SKU-SHAMPOO-250ML-LAVENDER`). If the company changes packaging vendors, alters the wall thickness, or switches from PET to rPET while keeping the product SKU identical, the DoC becomes disconnected from physical reality.
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `DoC Number: DOC-PPWR-2026-00481`  
  > `Packaging Reference Code: PKG-BTL-HDPE-250-REV03 (EAN Packaging Component Code: 4012345678901)`

---

### Item 2: Name and Address of the Manufacturer & Authorised Representative
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `Name and address of the manufacturer and, where applicable, the manufacturer’s authorised representative:`
* **Drafting Instruction:** State the full registered legal entity name, corporate registration number, and complete registered business address (street, number, postal code, city, country).
* **Who is the Manufacturer?:** Under Article 3(1)(13), the manufacturer is whoever designs, manufactures, or **has packaging designed or manufactured under their name or trademark**. For branded retail goods, this is the **brand owner**, not the third-party packaging converter.  
  *(Statutory Exception - Article 3(1)(13)(b) & Article 15(12)): If the brand owner is a micro-enterprise (under Recommendation 2003/361/EC) and its packaging supplier is located in the same EU Member State, the domestic packaging supplier is legally considered the manufacturer for compliance obligations.*
* **Authorised Representative (AR):** If a non-EU brand owner designates an EU AR under Article 17, the AR’s full EU name and legal address must be explicitly stated. If no AR is appointed, state: `Authorised Representative: Not Applicable`.
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `Manufacturer: Solaria Cosmetics Europe B.V.`  
  > `Address: Keizersgracht 421, 1016 EK Amsterdam, The Netherlands`  
  > `Company Registration No: NL882391044B01`  
  > `Authorised Representative: Not Applicable (EU established manufacturer)`

---

### Item 3: Statement of Sole Responsibility
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `This declaration of conformity is issued under the sole responsibility of the manufacturer.`
* **Drafting Instruction:** This is a fixed, non-negotiable statutory clause. **Do not modify, soften, or qualify this sentence**. Adding clauses like *"to the best of our knowledge"* or *"based solely on supplier data"* invalidates the legal value of the document.
* **Compliant Text (Exact phrasing required):**
  > `This declaration of conformity is issued under the sole responsibility of the manufacturer.`

---

### Item 4: Object of the Declaration (Traceability & Technical Description)
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `Object of the declaration (identification of the packaging allowing traceability): description of the packaging:`
* **Drafting Instruction:** Provide an unambiguous physical and technical description of the packaging unit and all its constituent packaging components. An enforcement inspector reading this section must be able to inspect a physical unit on a store shelf or warehouse and immediately match it to the DoC and the technical file.
* **Mandatory Data Points to Include:**
  1. Packaging category (Sales / Grouped / Transport).
  2. Structural format (e.g., rigid bottle, folding carton, multi-layer pouch, corrugated master box).
  3. Bill of constituent components (e.g., container body, cap/closure, tamper-evident band, self-adhesive label).
  4. Base materials and material identification codes pursuant to Decision 97/129/EC (applicable until 12 August 2028 per Article 70(2), transitioning to harmonised EU labelling under Article 12(6); e.g., HDPE 02, PP 05, PAP 20, PAP 21).
  5. Nominal dimensions and tare weight (in grams).
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `Object of Declaration: 250 ml Cylindrical Personal Care Bottle with Flip-Top Cap and Label.`  
  > `Packaging Function: Primary / Sales Packaging.`  
  > `Constituent Components & Materials:`  
  > `- Bottle Body: High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE 02), Tare Weight: 18.5g (incorporating 35% post-consumer recycled HDPE).`  
  > `- Flip-Top Dispensing Closure: Polypropylene (PP 05), Tare Weight: 4.2g (unpigmented, mono-material closure).`  
  > `- Front/Back Labels: Polyolefin film (PP 05) with alkali-washable acrylic adhesive, Tare Weight: 0.6g.`  
  > `Total Packaging System Tare Weight: 23.3g.`  
  > `Traceability Marker: Laser-etched batch code on base and 2D DataMatrix code printed on secondary shipping case.`

---

### Item 5: Relevant Union Harmonisation Legislation
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `The object of the declaration referred to in point 4 is in conformity with the relevant Union harmonisation legislation: … (reference to the other Union acts applied).`
* **Drafting Instruction:** State compliance with Regulation (EU) 2025/40. Furthermore, where the packaging falls under overlapping EU safety or environmental acts, list them explicitly.
* **Key Intersecting EU Acts:**
  - **Regulation (EU) 2025/40** on packaging and packaging waste (PPWR).
  - **Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 (REACH)** regarding SVHC candidate list substances and Annex XVII chemical restrictions.
  - **Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 & Regulation (EU) No 10/2011** (for food-contact packaging materials).
  - **Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS)** (where packaging forms part of an electrical/electronic equipment assembly).
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `The object of the declaration described in point 4 is in conformity with the relevant Union harmonisation legislation:`  
  > `1. Regulation (EU) 2025/40 of the European Parliament and of the Council on packaging and packaging waste (PPWR).`  
  > `2. Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning REACH (Substances of Very High Concern).`  
  > `3. Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 on materials and articles intended to come into contact with food (where applicable).`

---

### Item 6: Harmonised Standards, Common Specifications, & Technical Specifications
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `References to the relevant harmonised standards or the common specifications used or references to the other technical specifications in relation to which conformity is declared:`
* **Drafting Instruction:** 
  - Because formal CEN harmonised standards developed specifically under PPWR Articles 36 & 37 are phasing in between 2025 and 2028, manufacturers must cite the **existing harmonised European standards (EN standards)**, recognized technical specifications, and laboratory test protocols applied to demonstrate compliance with Articles 5–11.
* **Standard Citations by Requirement:**
  * **Article 5 (Requirements for Substances in Packaging & Heavy Metals):**  
    `EN 13428:2004` (Requirements specific to manufacturing and composition - Prevention by source reduction).  
    `EN IEC 63000:2018` (Technical documentation for the assessment of electrical and electronic products with respect to the restriction of hazardous substances).  
    `EPA Method 537.1 / DIN EN ISO 21675` (PFAS testing protocols ensuring compliance with Article 5(5) limits for food-contact packaging: < 25 ppb targeted PFAS, < 250 ppb sum of targeted PFAS, < 50 ppm total PFAS / total fluorine limit from 12 August 2026).
  * **Article 6 (Recyclable Packaging & Design for Recycling):**  
    `EN 13430:2004` (Packaging - Requirements for packaging recoverable by material recycling).  
    `RecyClass / CEFLEX Design for Recycling Guidelines` (D-f-R protocol assessment yielding Class A/B/C recyclability rating per Annex II Table 3).
  * **Article 7 (Minimum Recycled Content in Plastic Packaging):**  
    `EN 15343:2007` (Plastics recycling traceability and assessment of conformity and recycled content).  
    `ISO 22095:2020` (Chain of custody - General terminology and models).
  * **Article 8 (Biobased Feedstock in Plastic Packaging):**  
    `EN 16640 / EN 16785-1` (Bio-based content determination, where biobased plastics are utilised).
  * **Article 9 (Compostable Packaging):**  
    `EN 13432:2000` (Requirements for packaging recoverable through composting and biodegradation).  
    `EN 14995:2006` (Plastics - Evaluation of compostability). Applicable to fruit/vegetable sticky labels, tea/coffee pods, and very lightweight plastic bags pursuant to Article 9 and Annex III.
  * **Article 10 & 24 (Packaging Minimisation & Empty Space Ratio):**  
    `EN 13428:2004` (Assessment of packaging weight and volume minimisation to critical performance limits per Annex IV).  
    CAD volumetric ratio calculation confirming empty space ratio $\le 50\%$ for grouped, transport, and e-commerce packaging under Article 24.
  * **Article 11 & 26 (Reusable Packaging & Re-use Systems):**  
    `EN 13429:2004` (Packaging - Requirements for reusable packaging and system rotations conforming to Annex VI).
  * **Article 12 (Labelling of Packaging):**  
    Harmonised sorting markings, material identification, and digital data carriers (QR code) enabling consumer access to waste sorting and re-use information.
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `References to harmonised standards, common specifications, and technical standards applied:`  
  > `- Substance Restrictions (Art. 5): EN 13428:2004 (Section 6.2 - Heavy metal limits: Pb + Cd + Hg + Cr(VI) < 100 mg/kg verified via ICP-OES test report TR-SGS-2026-991); PFAS non-detection tested under DIN EN ISO 21675.`  
  > `- Recyclability Assessment (Art. 6): EN 13430:2004; RecyClass Recyclability Evaluation Protocol for HDPE Containers (Assessment Score: 96% - Class A).`  
  > `- Recycled Content Verification (Art. 7): EN 15343:2007 (Audit Certificate CoC-EU-8841-2026).`  
  > `- Packaging Minimisation (Art. 10 & Annex IV): EN 13428:2004 (Design Dossier Justification DD-SOL-250ML).`

---

### Item 7: Notified Body Assessment
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `Where applicable, the notified body … (name, address, number) … performed … (description of intervention) … and issued the certificate(s): …`
* **Drafting Instruction:** 
  - Packaging conformity assessment under the PPWR is primarily governed by **Module A (Internal Production Control)** under Annex VII. Module A is a **self-assessment procedure** that does not require third-party notified body intervention.
  - If no notified body was legally mandated or contracted, write: `Not Applicable (Internal Production Control under Annex VII Module A)`.
  - Only populate this section if a third-party conformity assessment body was formally engaged for certified module audits.
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `Not Applicable. Conformity assessment carried out by the manufacturer in accordance with Module A (Internal Production Control) as specified in Annex VII of Regulation (EU) 2025/40.`

---

### Item 8: Additional Information, Place/Date, and Signatory
* **Exact Regulatory Requirement:** `Additional information: Signed for and on behalf of: (place and date of issue): (name, function) (signature):`
* **Drafting Instruction:**
  1. **Supporting Documentation Reference:** Cross-reference the specific Annex VII Technical File reference number.
  2. **Retention Statement:** State the commitment to retain the dossier for the required statutory period (5 years single-use / 10 years reusable).
  3. **Place and Date:** City of signing and exact date (DD/MM/YYYY). Must be dated **prior** to the commercial placement of the packaging batch on the EU market.
  4. **Signatory:** Printed full name, legal corporate title (e.g., Managing Director, VP of Regulatory Affairs, Chief Compliance Officer), and handwritten or qualified electronic signature (QES under eIDAS).
* **Compliant Example:**
  > `Additional Information:`  
  > `Supporting Technical Dossier Reference: TF-SOL-PKG-2026-00481 (Maintained at corporate headquarters for minimum 5 years).`  
  > `Signed for and on behalf of: Solaria Cosmetics Europe B.V.`  
  > `Place and date of issue: Amsterdam, The Netherlands — 18 August 2026`  
  > `Name: Dr. Henrik Van Der Meer`  
  > `Function: Director of Quality Assurance & Regulatory Compliance`  
  > `Signature: [Qualified Electronic Signature / Stamp]`

## 3. Translation & Language Mandates (Article 39(2))

A critical compliance trap under Article 39(2):
* The DoC must be drawn up in or **translated into the official language or languages required by the Member State** in which the packaging is placed on the market.
* While the master technical documentation may be kept in English (unless national authorities specifically request a translation for cause), **the Declaration of Conformity itself must be available in the local market language**.
* Multi-language DoCs (such as the JBL and Koopman models) or dynamic digital portals that generate the official language text based on destination country are the industry gold standard.
