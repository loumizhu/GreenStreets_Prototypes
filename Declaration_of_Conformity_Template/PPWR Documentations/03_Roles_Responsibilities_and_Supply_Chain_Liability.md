# Roles, Responsibilities & Supply Chain Liability under PPWR
**Legal actor classification, delegation boundaries, and commercial risk distribution**

---

## 1. Legal Actor Classification Matrix

A primary cause of regulatory non-compliance under the PPWR is the confusion between **packaging manufacturers (converters)** and **brand owners/packagers**. 

Article 3(1)(13) establishes a single, clear rule determining who carries the manufacturer’s statutory burden:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               LEGAL DEFINITION OF "MANUFACTURER" (PPWR Art. 3(1)(13))             |
|                                                                                   |
| "Any natural or legal person that manufactures packaging or a packaged product;   |
|  however:                                                                         |
|  (a) where a person has packaging or a packaged product designed or               |
|      manufactured under its own name or trademark, that person is manufacturer;   |
|  (b) MICRO-ENTERPRISE CARVE-OUT (Art. 3(1)(13)(b) & Art. 15(12)):                 |
|      Where a brand owner is a micro-enterprise (<10 staff, <= €2M turnover)       |
|      and its packaging supplier is located in the same Member State, the          |
|      SUPPLIER is legally considered the manufacturer for PPWR compliance!"        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### Actor Breakdown & Statutory Obligations

| Supply Chain Role | Physical Activity | PPWR Legal Role | Mandatory Obligations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brand Owner / Private Label Retailer** | Designs, specifies, or purchases packaging to sell their branded goods inside the EU. | **MANUFACTURER (Primary Obligor, Art. 15)** | **Full Responsibility:** Must draw up Annex VII technical dossier, perform Article 38 conformity assessment, sign Annex VIII DoC, and retain records for 5/10 years. *(Unless micro-enterprise buying from domestic EU supplier under Art. 3(1)(13)(b)).* |
| **Packaging Converter / Supplier** | Extrudes, moulds, prints, or fabricates physical boxes, bottles, films, or pallets. | **SUPPLIER (Art. 16)** *(or Manufacturer under Art. 3(1)(13)(b) for micro-enterprises)* | **Statutory Duty to Furnish Evidence (Art. 16):** Must provide the manufacturer with all technical documentation and test reports necessary to demonstrate compliance with Articles 5 to 11 and Annex VII. |
| **Contract Packer / Toll Filler** | Packs product into packaging specified by the brand owner. | **OPERATOR** | Must ensure packaging handling and storage conditions do not jeopardise compliance (seals, barrier integrity, labelling visibility). |
| **Importer (Non-EU Goods)** | Places packaged products from outside the EU onto the EU market. | **IMPORTER (Art. 18 & Art. 21)** | Must verify that the non-EU manufacturer carried out conformity assessment and drew up Annex VII technical files. Must keep DoC copy for 5/10 years. Under **Art. 21**, becomes Manufacturer if importing under own name/brand. |
| **Distributor / Wholesaler / Retailer** | Distributes packaged products on the market without altering the packaging. | **DISTRIBUTOR (Art. 19 & Art. 21)** | Due diligence: Must verify producer EPR registration (Art. 44), labelling (Art. 12), and traceability markings (Art. 15(5)-(6)). Under **Art. 21**, becomes Manufacturer if relabelling/modifying packaging. |
| **Fulfillment Service Provider (FSP)** | Warehouses, packs, addresses, and dispatches goods for e-commerce. | **FULFILMENT SERVICE PROVIDER (Art. 20)** | Must ensure warehousing, handling, and packing do not jeopardise compliance with Articles 5–12. When packing, must strictly enforce the $\le 50\%$ empty space limit under **Article 24**. |

## 2. The Authorised Representative (Article 17 vs. Article 40/44)

The PPWR distinguishes between two different types of Authorised Representatives:
1. **Authorised Representative for Product Conformity (Article 17):** Appointed by a non-EU manufacturer via written mandate to handle regulatory compliance, DoC retention, and market surveillance inquiries.
2. **Authorised Representative for EPR (Article 3(1)(20) & Article 44/45):** Appointed in each Member State to fulfil national register obligations and extended producer responsibility fees.

### 2.1 Statutory Mandate Scope under Article 17(2)
The written mandate **shall allow** the AR to do at least the following:
* Keep the EU Declaration of Conformity and technical documentation at the disposal of market surveillance authorities for **5 years** (single-use) or **10 years** (reusable).
* Provide competent authorities, upon reasoned request, with all information and documentation in an easily understood language within **10 days**.
* Cooperate with national authorities on corrective actions.
* Terminate the mandate if the manufacturer acts contrary to its PPWR obligations.

### 2.2 Statutory Limitations (What CANNOT Be Delegated)
> [!CAUTION]
> **Article 17(2), closing paragraph, strictly prohibits delegating the core engineering duty:**  
> *"The obligations laid down in Article 15(1) and the obligation to draw up the technical documentation referred to in Annex VII and required under or pursuant to Articles 5 to 11 shall not form part of the authorised representative’s mandate."*  
> The manufacturer itself must establish the technical file and execute the conformity assessment. An AR cannot "invent" or certify compliance independently without the manufacturer's engineering file.

## 3. Supply Chain Contracting & Data Pass-Through

Because brand owners legally sign the DoC while converters hold the underlying chemical and polymer formulations, **contractual compliance mechanics** are essential.

### Recommended Commercial Safeguards
1. **PPWR Data Clause in Procurement Contracts:** Mandate that packaging suppliers must supply complete Bill of Materials (BOM) breakdowns, material codes (97/129/EC), and independent lab test reports (Pb/Cd/Hg/Cr(VI) < 100 ppm; PFAS non-detection) within 15 calendar days of request.
2. **Notification of Formulation Changes:** Suppliers must contractually agree not to modify resins, colorants, barrier coatings, or adhesive chemistries without 90 days prior written notice, as any alteration invalidates the existing DoC.
3. **Indemnity for Misrepresented Compliance:** Contractual indemnification against regulatory fines, product recalls, or stock quarantine resulting from inaccurate supplier material disclosures.
