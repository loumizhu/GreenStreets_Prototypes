Who it concerns

PPWR applies to more than the shop

If you make, import, distribute or sell packaged goods in the EU, your packaging data matters. Whatever your size, wherever you sit in the chain, the right information needs to move with the product.

  1. ManufacturerDeclares what the packaging is made of
  2. ImporterNeeds it before the goods enter the EU
  3. DistributorPasses it on with every shipment
  4. RetailerAsks for the data, and answers to the regulator

The same product passes through every one of them. Each step depends on accurate packaging data — and each connection is a chance to make the next request easier.
