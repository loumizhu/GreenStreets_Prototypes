# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing codebase: plain static HTML/CSS/JS prototypes, no build step. The landing page is a single self-contained file, `Landing_Page/hero.html`.

## Users
Everyone in the EU packaged-goods chain, with equal weight: manufacturers, importers, distributors, retailers and their suppliers. A retailer-side compliance lead needs packaging data collected from many suppliers; a supplier or brand owner is being asked for the same data by many customers. (Confirmed: both sides, equal weight.)

## Product Purpose
Greenstreets keeps PPWR (EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation) packaging data ready, complete and easy to share, while building a stronger network of customers and suppliers. Success is a visitor booking a demo.

## Positioning
The chain grows: when a customer invites you in, you can invite your suppliers too, so each new connection makes the next data request easier. (From the copywriting doc.) Core pillars: Packaging Library (fill in once, share in each customer's format), Supplier Hub (send requests, see what is missing, audit trail), Network growth.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from retailer invites or from search on PPWR. They handle packaging records per component: material, weight, recyclability, recycled content, supplier data.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Landing copy is client-owned (`Landing_Page/Landing Page Copywriting.docx`). **Do not rewrite headlines.** The five hero proposals stay as written, and the reviewer bar that switches them stays.
- Lede, audience line and CTAs ("See how it works" / "Book a demo") come from the same doc.
- Page uses `../img/Logo-WG.png` and `../img/greenstreet-favicon.png`.

## Brand Commitments
Greenstreets name and logo (`img/Logo-WG.png`), brand green accent. The doc says the demo "takes 20 minutes".

## Evidence on Hand
No customer counts, logos, testimonials or dates are confirmed. Do not fabricate them: any proof slot ships as a clearly marked placeholder. The PPWR application date may only be cited from the official 2025/40 text with excerpt and PDF page.

## Product Principles
1. Show the product doing its job with real packaging fields, not generic SaaS decoration.
2. The chain-growth mechanism is the differentiator and should be visible.
3. Never invent commercial or factual claims.
4. Both sides of the chain are first-class visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard operable, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, one `main` landmark, working at 375px.
